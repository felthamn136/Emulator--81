# Copyright and ROM Distribution

The ZX81 ROM remains copyrighted material and is not covered by this project's source-code licence. Historical documentation indicates that the original ZX81 ROM was created by Nine Tiles, and its copyright position is separate from Amstrad's documented permission concerning ZX Spectrum ROMs.

This project does not claim ownership of the original ZX81 ROM. Publicly available Sinclair preservation resources identify the ZX80 and ZX81 ROMs as copyright Nine Tiles and report that those ROMs have been distributed “with kind permission”.

Any original ZX81 ROM image included with or distributed alongside this project is therefore provided in good faith on the basis of this reported permission. The ROM remains the intellectual property of its respective copyright holder, and no rights to the ROM are granted by the licence covering this project's source code. Existing copyright notices must not be removed or altered.

Although historical and preservation sources provide evidence that permission has been granted for the distribution of ZX81 ROM material in at least some circumstances, I have not located the original permission correspondence or documentation establishing its precise scope and terms. This project therefore does not represent that such permission constitutes an unrestricted licence applicable to every form of redistribution.

If you are a copyright holder or an authorised representative and believe that any ROM or other copyrighted material distributed with this project infringes your rights, please contact the project maintainer. The matter will be reviewed and, where appropriate, the material will be removed or replaced.

## References

[Sinclair ZX80 / ZX81 ROMs information file, spectrumforeveryone/zx-roms](https://github.com/spectrumforeveryone/zx-roms/blob/main/zx80-81/info.txt)

Identifies the ZX80/ZX81 ROMs as copyright Nine Tiles Inc and explicitly states that they are“used with kind permission”. This is the most directly relevant reference for the ZX81 permission statement.

[World of Spectrum Classic: Copyright and Distribution Permissions](https://worldofspectrum.net/permits/)

Provides historical background concerning copyright and distribution permissions in the Sinclair preservation and emulation community. It specifically records free distribution permission for ZX Spectrum ROMs, rather than establishing permission for the ZX81 ROM.

[Document Amstrad/Sinclair ROM permission and attribution, spec_chum issue #189](https://github.com/mward-sudo/spec_chum/issues/189)

A secondary, non-authoritative research summary of the documented Amstrad position. Importantly, it records that the Amstrad permission applied to Spectrum ROMs and did not cover the ZX80/ZX81.

[ZX Spectrum Next: licences and attribution](https://ide.retrogamecoders.com/zxnext-about.html)

Provides an example of ROM licensing and attribution in a modern Sinclair-related project and documents the separate Amstrad/Sky permission relied upon for relevant ZX Spectrum ROM images. It should be regarded as contextual evidence rather than evidence of ZX81 permission.
